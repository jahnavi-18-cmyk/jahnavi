🏭 Normal Distribution

Manufacturing Quality Control

1. Industry Problem

A factory manufactures metal shafts with a target diameter of 10 mm. Due to manufacturing variation, shaft diameters differ slightly. The normal distribution helps engineers understand this variation and estimate the percentage of defective products.

2. Learning Objectives

  • Understand the bell-shaped curve.
  • Learn the role of mean and standard deviation.
  • Understand manufacturing tolerances.
  • Estimate defective production rates.

3. Mathematical Formula

f(x) = 1 / (σ√2π) × e−(x−μ)² / 2σ²

Interval probability:

P(L ≤ X ≤ U) = Φ((U−μ)/σ) − Φ((L−μ)/σ)

μ = Mean diameter (mm)
σ = Standard deviation (mm)
x = Selected diameter (mm)
Φ = Standard normal CDF

📦 Central Limit Theorem

Warehouse Processing Time

1. Industry Problem

A warehouse processes customer orders. Processing times vary from order to order. The Central Limit Theorem explains how sample averages tend to follow a normal distribution when sample size increases, under suitable conditions.

2. Learning Objectives

  • Understand sample means.
  • Learn the importance of sample size.
  • Understand sampling distributions.

3. Mathematical Formula

Z = (X̄ − μ) / (σ / √n)

X̄ = Sample mean
μ = Population mean
σ = Population standard deviation
n = Sample size
