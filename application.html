Normal Distribution — Manufacturing Quality Control

A manufacturing company produces metal shafts with a target diameter of 10 mm. Due to manufacturing variations, the actual diameter follows a normal distribution.

The company accepts shafts between 9.90 mm and 10.10 mm. Shafts outside these limits are considered defective.

Use probability calculations to estimate the percentage of acceptable and defective products.

Mathematical Formula

Normal Probability Density Function
f(x) = 1 / (σ√(2π)) × e−(x−μ)² / (2σ²)

Standardization
Z = (X − μ) / σ

Interval Probability
P(a ≤ X ≤ b) = Φ(zb) − Φ(za)
