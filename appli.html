3. PMF Calculator

PMF calculates the probability of exactly k successes.

P(X = k) = C(n,k) pk (1-p)(n-k)

4. CDF Calculator

CDF calculates the probability of k or fewer successes.

P(X ≤ k) —
P(X > k) = 1 − P(X ≤ k) —

P(X ≤ k) means the probability of getting k or fewer successes.

The graph displays P(X) for X = 0, 1, 2, ..., n. Hover over a bar to see its probability. The dashed vertical line represents the mean.
